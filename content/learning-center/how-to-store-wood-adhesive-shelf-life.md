Water-based wood adhesives and polymer emulsions are stable products — when they're stored correctly. Heat, sunlight, freezing and open lids can shorten shelf life, change viscosity or even ruin a whole drum. Here's how dealers, workshops and factories should store them.

## Shelf life basics

**Enfixx wood adhesives have a shelf life of 12 months** when stored properly in a cool, shaded place. Other emulsions have shelf lives stated on their technical data sheets. Shelf life assumes:

- **Unopened, original containers**
- **Cool, shaded, dry storage**
- **No freezing and no prolonged high temperatures**

## The five storage rules

1. **Keep out of direct sunlight.** Sun-baked drums on a shop front or rooftop can heat far above ambient, causing skinning, viscosity rise or separation.
2. **Avoid heat sources.** Store away from boilers, hot presses, ovens and metal roofs.
3. **Never let it freeze.** Freezing can permanently destabilise an emulsion — polymer particles coagulate into lumps. This matters for transport through cold regions in winter.
4. **Close lids tightly** after every use. Air exposure forms skins and lets the product dry at the surface.
5. **First in, first out.** Rotate stock so older batches are used first. Mark receipt dates on containers.

## For dealers and hardware stores

- Stack tubs and drums **indoors, off the floor** on pallets or racks.
- Don't display adhesive in shop windows exposed to afternoon sun.
- Keep a simple **batch and date log** — it helps with FIFO and any customer queries.

## For factories using drums and IBCs

- Store drums **upright and sealed**; use a drum pump or tap instead of opening lids repeatedly.
- **Stir gently** before use if the TDS recommends it — avoid entraining air.
- Don't pour unused glue from spreaders back into the main drum; it may be contaminated or partially dried.
- Clean spreaders and machine rollers with water before adhesive dries on them.

## How to check a stored adhesive before use

- **Look:** smooth and uniform, no hard lumps, no thick skin, no unusual colour.
- **Smell:** no sour or rotten odour (a sign of microbial spoilage).
- **Feel:** viscosity similar to fresh product; no stringy or gritty texture.
- **Test:** glue two offcuts and break the joint after curing. Strong wood tear = good to use.

If a product has frozen, has hard lumps, smells spoiled or has passed its shelf life, don't use it on customer work — contact the supplier.

## Key takeaways

- **Cool, shaded, sealed, never frozen.**
- Enfixx: **12-month shelf life** under proper storage.
- Rotate stock **FIFO** and log batch dates.
- **Check before use** — appearance, smell, viscosity and a quick bond test.
