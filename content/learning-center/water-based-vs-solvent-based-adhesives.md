Walk past a workshop using solvent-based contact adhesive and you'll smell it from the street. Walk past one using a water-based synthetic resin adhesive and you won't. That difference — **volatile organic compounds (VOCs)** — is reshaping adhesives, paints and coatings worldwide. Here's how water-based and solvent-based systems compare.

## What's the difference?

- **Solvent-based** products dissolve or disperse the polymer in **organic solvents** (such as toluene, hexane, acetone or aromatic blends). The solvent evaporates to leave the film or bond.
- **Water-based** products (emulsions, dispersions, latexes) carry polymer particles in **water**. Water evaporates or is absorbed, and the particles coalesce into a film.

## Side-by-side

| Factor | Water-based | Solvent-based |
|---|---|---|
| VOC emissions | Low | High |
| Odour | Low | Strong |
| Fire risk | Very low | High (flammable vapours) |
| Worker exposure | Low | Requires ventilation and PPE |
| Clean-up | Water (when wet) | Solvents |
| Drying speed | Slower on non-porous surfaces | Fast |
| Bonding non-porous surfaces | Needs suitable design | Often easier (contact adhesives) |
| Freeze sensitivity | Must not freeze | Generally not an issue |
| Regulatory trend | Favoured | Increasingly restricted |

## Why the industry is moving to water-based

- **Health and safety:** less solvent exposure for carpenters, painters and factory workers, and occupants of homes and offices.
- **Fire safety:** no flammable vapour build-up in workshops and warehouses.
- **Indoor air quality:** lower emissions after installation — important for homes, schools and hospitals.
- **Regulation and brand requirements:** VOC limits for paints and adhesives are tightening in many markets.
- **Performance improvements:** modern emulsion polymers now match or beat solvent systems in many applications, including water-resistant **D3 wood adhesives** and high-durability **exterior paints**.

## Where solvent systems still appear

Solvent-based contact adhesives remain common for some laminate, foam, rubber and fabric bonding where instant grab without clamping is needed. For most **woodworking, laminate pressing, paints, construction chemicals, textile printing and coir** applications, water-based systems are the modern standard.

## Making the switch

- Match the **application method** — water-based adhesives need clamping or pressing for laminates.
- Allow for **drying time** in your process.
- **Protect from freezing** in transport and storage.
- **Train teams** on open time and spreading.

## Key takeaways

- Water-based systems are **safer, lower-VOC and lower-odour**.
- Modern emulsions deliver **high performance**, including D3 water resistance and exterior durability.
- Solvent systems still have niches, but the trend is clearly **water-based**.
